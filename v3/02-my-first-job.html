What to expect from your first job

Straight answers, so you know what you are walking into.

What you need

  • To be 18 or over
  • To turn up on time
  • Basic English or French
  • A willingness to learn

What you do not need

  • ✗ Previous experience
  • ✗ A finished CV
  • ✗ A diploma or certificate
  • ✗ Your own transport
What it pays

Most first roles start between Rs 14,000 and Rs 19,000 a month. Transport is often paid on top.

The hours

Shops and hotels work shifts, weekends included. Offices are usually 8.30 to 4.30, Monday to Friday.

Getting there

Most first jobs are a bus ride away. Check the route before you apply, it matters more than the salary.

Your first week

You will be shown everything, working next to someone experienced. Nobody expects you to know the job yet.
