Shop Assistant, Bagatelle

Kendra Retail Ltd

MokaRs 14,000 to Rs 17,000 Shifts, 5 days a weekPosted Sep 8, 2026Closing 12/10/2026

About the role

You will work on the shop floor at our Bagatelle store, helping customers find what they need, keeping displays tidy and handling the till. You will be trained on everything in your first two weeks, alongside an experienced colleague.

Your day

  • Greet customers and help them choose
  • Keep shelves stocked and the floor presentable
  • Handle payments at the till
  • Help with deliveries and stock counts

Getting there and settling in

  • How you get here: buses from Curepipe, Rose Hill and Port Louis stop at the mall entrance, about five minutes from the store
  • Your break: 45 minutes, with a staff canteen on the lower level
  • Who you work with: a team of six on the floor plus a supervisor, most of them under 25
  • What you wear: uniform provided, you bring your own black shoes

What we offer

  • Rs 14,000 to Rs 17,000 depending on shift pattern
  • Transport allowance
  • Staff discount
  • Full training, no experience required

About Kendra Retail Ltd

Kendra operates 14 stores across Mauritius. Around a third of our shop team joined us as their first job, and several store managers started on the floor.
