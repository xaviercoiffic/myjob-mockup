MyJob design kit

Sampled directly from the live myjob.mu interface. Same navy, same aqua, same button gradients, extended with one accent per new section.

Logo

Taken straight from the MyJob 2.0 Figma file rather than redrawn. Three files ship with the prototype: the wordmark for light backgrounds, a white version for the navy header, and the M mark for the favicon and app icon.

Colour tokens

Every value was sampled pixel by pixel from the two screenshots of the live site. Nothing is approximated. The two section accents that did not already exist, coral and steel, are darkened versions of colours that do: the search button orange and the header navy.

Typography

Nunito for headings and Nunito Sans for body. The rounded terminals match the existing MyJob wordmark and read young without becoming childish, which matters when the same design system also has to carry hotel and trade sections.

Form elements

Inputs are 13px by 15px with a 2px border that turns teal on focus. Radius 12px on fields, pill on search bars. Labels are always present, visually or for screen readers only.

Spacing and radius

8px base scale. Sections use 64px vertical padding on desktop, 40px on mobile. Cards use 20px to 24px padding.
