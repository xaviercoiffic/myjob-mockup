What to expect from your first job

Straight answers, so you know what you are walking into.

What it pays

Most first roles in retail, hospitality and admin start between Rs 14,000 and Rs 19,000 a month, plus transport in many cases.

The hours

Shops and hotels work in shifts, including weekends. Office roles are usually 8.30 to 4.30, Monday to Friday.

What employers look for

Turning up on time, speaking politely to customers and being willing to learn. Experience is not the first thing on the list.

How to apply well

Fill your profile properly, add a photo, and say in one line why you want the job. That alone puts you ahead of most applicants.
